# Interview guide

*The interview is step 2 of a [successful career](../README.md#6--a-successful-career) — [being picked](../career/career-traits.md) — made formal. [Being known](../career/being-known.md) is what got you into the room; inside it, the job is to show three things: the abilities, the track record, and the traits. Everything in this repo has an Interview lens for that reason; this folder holds what cuts across them.*

Cross-cutting interview material. Stage-specific questions live in the [workflow page](../workflow.md); ability-specific behavioural questions live in `competencies/`. This folder holds the things that don't belong to one stage.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| [`behavioural-story-bank-template.md`](behavioural-story-bank-template.md) | STAR template, tagged by ability, disposition and trait, with the prompts to prepare |
| [`product-sense-cases.md`](product-sense-cases.md) | Worked product-sense / metric / business cases, each with a strong answer and common weak answers — *in progress* |
| [`sql-practice.md`](sql-practice.md) | SQL patterns that come up in analyst interviews, with worked solutions — *in progress* |
| [`experimentation-questions.md`](experimentation-questions.md) | A/B testing and causal-inference questions, with worked answers — *in progress* |

Until the three in-progress files are written, the stage-level interview lenses on the [workflow page](../workflow.md) and the question tables in [technical craft](../competencies/technical-craft.md#interview-lens) and [analytical reasoning](../competencies/analytical-reasoning.md#interview-lens) cover the same ground.
