# Where are you?

The workflow and the abilities describe the job. These four pages describe *you* at a point in your working life, and route you through the rest of the repo accordingly. They add no new theory — each is a reading path plus the handful of things that are specific to that phase.

| Phase | You are… | Start with |
|---|---|---|
| [Becoming](becoming.md) | Entering the field, or switching into it | The whole workflow once, then Stages 2, 4, 7 |
| [Working](working.md) | In the job, roughly your first three years | Stages 1, 3 and 8 — the ones nobody trained you for |
| [Advancing](advancing.md) | Moving from doing the work to shaping it | The senior column of every ability table |
| [Reproducing](reproducing.md) | Hiring, onboarding, mentoring, building a team | How to transmit the six abilities to others |

The phases roughly track the junior → mid → senior columns in each [competency](../competencies/) file, so those tables are the detail behind each door.
