## Career capital

*What makes a career go well is not only being good. It is being known to be good by the people who decide — and building that in a way that compounds.*

The mechanism is the same at every phase, but its weight shifts. Three things build it:

| | What it is | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| **Trust with people who matter** | A small number of people — fifty, not five thousand — who have seen you deliver and would vouch for you. Managers, senior peers, the people they ask. | Most good jobs are filled through a chain of people who know who actually did the work. Reputation inside a dense community moves you between companies; a large anonymous audience does not. |
| **Work others can see** | Shipped things with your name on them: an analysis that changed a decision, an internal tool others adopted, a public repo, a talk about a project that landed. | An artefact is proof, not claim. It vouches for you years later without an interview. Visibility that is a *by-product of the work* is stronger than visibility pursued for its own sake. |
| **Teaching and writing** | Documenting playbooks, onboarding others, internal talks; then external writing or speaking when there is something worth saying. | You cannot explain what you half understand, so teaching deepens the skill — and it leaves a trail that others find. |

Read down the phases and the emphasis moves from the first row to the third:

| Phase | Career capital looks like |
|---|---|
| [Becoming](becoming.md) | One end-to-end case in a public repo. Being known to a few people as someone who finishes things. |
| [Working](working.md) | A decision log with outcomes. A manager and two senior peers who would hire you again. Work done in the open — reviewed, documented, findable. |
| [Advancing](advancing.md) | A track record people cite. Being the person others come to before they have formed the question. Internal teaching: playbooks, reviews, talks. |
| [Reproducing](reproducing.md) | People you developed who have gone on to do well. Public work — writing, speaking, a body of shared knowledge — because by now there is something worth sharing. |

> **The one thing to remember:** the audience that matters is small and specific. Build trust with them through work they can see, and let the wider footprint grow from that — not the other way round.

The disposition that drives all of this is [working in the open](../dispositions.md#the-seven).
